# Voxel Tile Prompts

## County tile kit
Create a high-quality voxel tile kit for Atlas, a county intelligence app. Include: suburban residential tile, commercial plaza tile, warehouse/industrial tile, freeway road tile, city center tile, opportunity marker, scouted marker, campaign quest marker. Dark console-compatible colors, emerald/yellow/red/blue overlays, isometric, clean silhouettes, original assets.

## Riverside county demo board
Create a stylized isometric voxel county board inspired by Inland Empire geography, with city clusters, freeway ribbons, suburban zones, commercial corridors, hills, and water edges. It should feel like a premium strategy-game map, not a real Google Maps clone. No labels baked into image.
