# Atlas Pricing Model

## Core principle

Free users still get Clawd.

The paid wall is not "unlock the pet." The paid wall is "host Clawd as a daemon."

## Free: Clawd Companion

Free users can:
- Explore voxel county maps
- Ask basic local data questions
- Drop Clawd in limited locations
- Generate basic scout reports
- Preview campaigns
- Customize Clawd lightly
- Use demo/session XP

Suggested limits:
- 3 Scout Drops per week
- 1 temporary business profile
- 1 active map session
- Campaign preview only
- No evidence ledger
- No saved campaign history
- No weekly reports
- No exports

## Paid: Hosted Clawd Daemon — $20/month

Paid users get:
- Persistent Clawd
- Saved business profile
- Saved Scout Drops
- Saved campaigns
- Campaign quests
- Evidence tracking
- Real XP ledger
- Campaign asset library
- QR/flyer/post/DM generation
- Weekly scout report
- Exports
- More Scout Drops

Suggested limits:
- 25 Scout Drops/month
- 5 active campaigns
- 3 business profiles
- 3 active counties
- 100 campaign quests
- 100 saved assets
- Evidence history saved for 12 months

Title: Host Clawd for this business

CTA: Host Clawd — $20/month
