# Campaign Engine Spec

## Purpose

Turn Scout Drop output into a local marketing campaign.

## Campaign output

- Campaign name
- Target area
- Target customer
- Offer
- Channels
- 7-day plan
- Flyer copy
- QR landing page copy
- Social post captions
- DM scripts
- Partnership pitch
- Tracking checklist
- Quest preview

## Business templates

### Mobile detailing

Targets:
- residential driveways
- gyms
- apartments
- office lots
- dealership partnerships
- neighborhood groups

Assets:
- QR flyer
- Facebook post
- property manager DM
- first-time offer
- tracking checklist

### Cleaning service

Targets:
- apartments
- property managers
- small offices
- realtors
- move-in/move-out clusters

Assets:
- flyer
- cold email
- DM script
- offer sheet
- referral copy

### Local event/tournament

Targets:
- schools/colleges
- gaming cafés
- Discord/community groups
- flyers/QR
- venues
- local creators

Assets:
- event poster copy
- social captions
- DM script
- partner pitch
- QR page copy

## Anti-spam rules

- Do not auto-post.
- Do not auto-DM.
- Do not scrape private individuals.
- Do not target sensitive traits.
- Do not guarantee ROI.
- Ask for user confirmation before any action outside Atlas.
