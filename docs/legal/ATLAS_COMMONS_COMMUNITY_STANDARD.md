# Atlas Commons community standard

Status: draft awaiting named owner approval before any public production launch.

Atlas Commons is for short, place-specific notes that help someone understand a location. Public notes are reviewed before they are visible.

## Keep notes useful

- Write about the place you selected: access, timing, wayfinding, atmosphere, or a practical local observation.
- Use your own words and keep it specific enough to help another person.
- Treat public notes as public. Do not include anything you would not want displayed after review.

## Do not post

- personal contact details, precise home/work routines, account credentials, financial details, or other sensitive personal information;
- hate, threats, harassment, sexual content, or content targeting a protected trait;
- false accusations, doxxing, impersonation, or content that could put someone at risk;
- spam, advertising, referral links, scams, malware, or repeated off-topic posts;
- copied provider data, copyrighted text you do not have permission to share, or material that violates another platform's rules; or
- emergency requests. Atlas Commons is not an emergency service.

## Review, reports, and removals

- A public note is visible only after moderation approval.
- Anyone can report a visible note. Reports are reviewed according to the approved abuse-response procedure.
- Moderators may reject, hide, remove, restore, or limit a note or account identifier when needed to enforce this standard or protect the service.
- If you need a public note removed, contact mzwin3545@gmail.com with the note link or the selected place and approximate posting time. Do not send sensitive information by email.

## What this does not do

This standard does not replace the approved retention policy, privacy policy, or incident-escalation procedure. Those remain separate owner-gated launch requirements.
