## Visual Direction

- Identity: Field atlas
- Materials: Warm paper and ink, developed beyond a flat theme through authored texture, line, and motion studies
- Image-generation direction: Use Higgsfield for bounded visual exploration and source references; generated decoration must not obscure county geometry, map interaction, or truthfulness
