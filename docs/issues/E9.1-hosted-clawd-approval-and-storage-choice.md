# E9.1 Hosted Clawd Approval And Storage Choice

## Summary

Prepare the human approval packet for Hosted Clawd persistence. This is a
planning and decision slice only.

M1 Curated Alpha with session-only Clawd behavior.

## Target Maturity

M3 Persisted Beta after approval.

`HUMAN_APPROVAL_BEFORE_PERSISTENCE`

`HUMAN_APPROVAL_BEFORE_MONEY`

## Anti-Scope

- No database migrations.
- No auth provider setup.
- No database client.
- No Stripe Products, Prices, Checkout Sessions, Customer Portal Sessions, or
  webhooks.
- No saved Scout Drops or campaigns.
- No evidence upload/storage.
- No XP grants.
- No weekly reports.
- No exports.
- No automated posting, DMs, ads, scraping, or scheduled outreach.

## Acceptance

1. Storage provider decision options are documented.
2. Auth/account ownership decision is documented.
3. First persisted capability is recommended.
4. Required ownership, unauthenticated-denial, idempotency, and usage-limit tests
   are listed before implementation.
5. Stripe remains downstream of persistence unless the money gate is explicitly
   approved.
6. Evidence and XP remain out of the first persistence implementation.
7. Session-to-hosted promotion rules are explicit.
8. Usage-limit decisions are separated from suggested pricing limits.
9. A ready-to-use implementation prompt exists for the next agent after human
   approval.
