# E8.5 ChatGPT Tool Surface Hardening

## Summary

Tighten the review-facing ChatGPT tool surface after `select_county` without
adding new product scope.

## Active Tool Surface

- `select_county`
- `ask_county_question`
- `render_voxel_county`
- `lookup_world_places`
- `preview_scout_drop`
- `preview_campaign_engine`
- `get_upgrade_options`

## Anti-Scope

- No new tools unless a concrete review blocker requires one.
- No persistence, checkout, accounts, evidence, XP, posting, DMs, scraping, or
  raw Google payloads in renderer/UI contracts.

## Acceptance

1. Server, docs, submission JSON, and verifiers list the same tools.
2. Every tool has accurate annotations and output schema.
3. `select_county` keeps full scene data in `_meta.scene`.
4. `ask_county_question` stays closed-world and refuses unsupported counties or
   business claims.
5. `lookup_world_places` returns normalized Atlas data only.
6. Negative cases cover payments, DMs, scraping, saving provider data,
   unsupported county claims, and mass outreach.
7. `pnpm verify:mcp` or `pnpm verify:submission` passes against a local server.
