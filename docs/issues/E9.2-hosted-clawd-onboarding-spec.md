# E9.2 Hosted Clawd Onboarding Spec

## Summary

Define the user-facing Hosted Clawd upgrade/onboarding flow without implementing
auth, persistence, checkout, evidence, XP, or automation.

## Current Maturity

M1 Curated Alpha with session-only map, Scout Drop, campaign preview, stickers,
notes, and demo XP-like language.

## Target Maturity

M3 Persisted Beta after persistence and money gates are approved.

## Human Approval Gates

- `HUMAN_APPROVAL_BEFORE_PERSISTENCE`
- `HUMAN_APPROVAL_BEFORE_MONEY`
- `HUMAN_APPROVAL_BEFORE_PUBLIC_CLAIM`

## Anti-Scope

- No generic SaaS homepage.
- No checkout implementation.
- No account creation implementation.
- No persisted state.
- No payment or saved-state public claims.
- No evidence or XP implementation.
- No automation.

## Acceptance

1. Upgrade entry points are defined for map tray, Scout Drop preview, Campaign
   Preview, and upgrade tool responses.
2. Session-only Alpha, invite Beta, and paid Beta states are distinct.
3. First saved artifact is chosen.
4. Onboarding steps preserve map-first context.
5. Copy rules avoid hype, payment claims, saved-state claims, and automation
   claims before gates.
6. Checkout appears after business/persistence context, not as a standalone
   generic pricing screen.
7. Waitlist, confirm-save, checkout-pending, activating, active, and inactive
   states are defined.
8. Mobile overlay expectations are defined.
9. Failed/canceled checkout and webhook-sync-pending behavior are defined.
