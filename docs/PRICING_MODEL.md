# Atlas Pricing Model

Current maturity:
Pricing is a Beta planning contract only. Atlas Alpha must not start checkout,
create accounts, persist Hosted Clawd state, or imply paid access is live.

Human approval gate:
`HUMAN_APPROVAL_BEFORE_MONEY`.

## Core Principle

Free users still get Clawd.

The paid wall is not "unlock the pet." The paid wall is "host Clawd as a
daemon."

## Free: Clawd Companion

Free users can:

- Explore voxel county maps.
- Ask basic local data questions.
- Drop Clawd in limited locations.
- Generate basic scout reports.
- Preview campaigns.
- Customize Clawd lightly.
- Use demo/session XP.

Suggested limits:

- 3 Scout Drops per week.
- 1 temporary business profile.
- 1 active map session.
- Campaign preview only.
- No evidence ledger.
- No saved campaign history.
- No weekly reports.
- No exports.

## Paid: Hosted Clawd Daemon

Planning price:
$20/month.

Status:
Planned Beta, not available in Alpha.

Paid users get:

- Persistent Clawd.
- Saved business profile.
- Saved Scout Drops.
- Saved campaigns.
- Campaign quests.
- Evidence tracking.
- Real XP ledger.
- Campaign asset library.
- QR/flyer/post/script generation for manual use.
- Weekly scout report.
- Exports.
- More Scout Drops.

Suggested limits:

- 25 Scout Drops per month.
- 5 active campaigns.
- 3 business profiles.
- 3 active counties.
- 100 campaign quests.
- 100 saved assets.
- Evidence history saved for 12 months.

## Upgrade Copy

Title:
Host Clawd for this business

Alpha body:
You can explore with Clawd for free. Hosted Clawd is the planned Beta layer that
will save scout reports, remember a business profile, create campaign quests,
track evidence, and level Clawd up as you take action.

Alpha CTA:
Join Hosted Clawd waitlist

Beta CTA after approval:
Host Clawd - $20/month

## Copy Rules

- Do not say payment is available until Stripe/checkout is approved and built.
- Do not say progress is saved unless persistence is live for that user.
- Do not mix demo/session XP with real Hosted Clawd XP.
- Do not imply Atlas will post, DM, run ads, or automate outreach.
- Public pricing copy needs human approval before launch.
