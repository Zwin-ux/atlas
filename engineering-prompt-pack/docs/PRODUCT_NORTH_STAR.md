# Atlas Product North Star

Atlas is a voxel county intelligence app for ChatGPT.

Users can:
- explore counties visually
- ask local data questions
- drop Clawd into a location
- scout business opportunities
- generate local campaigns
- turn campaigns into quests
- submit evidence and level up Clawd

## Free

Clawd Companion:
- explore
- ask
- scout previews
- campaign previews

## Paid

Hosted Clawd Daemon:
- memory
- saved campaigns
- evidence
- quests
- XP
- weekly reports
- exports

## First killer demo

"Drop Clawd in Eastvale for a mobile detailing business."
